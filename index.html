WayV 威神V

Kun 钱锟

Kun is the leader of WayV. He is one of the main singers, and also has written lyrics and produced music for the group. His other interests include photography, video editing, cooking, travelling, and skydiving.

Fun fact: He can fly a plane!

Ten 李永钦

Ten is the main dancer of WayV and also a main singer. He has produced choreography for the group. His interests include dance, travel, and playing with his cats. He speaks 5 languages (Thai, Korean, Mandarin, English, Japanese). He is also a solo artist with two albums.

Fun Fact: He hates fruit.

Xiaojun 肖德俊

Xiaojun is a main singer of WayV. He has been in many musical variety shows and is very passionate about singing. He has a dog named Bella and comes from a musically talented family.

Fun Fact: He loves the Twilight Saga (hense the name "Bella")
